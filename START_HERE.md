## 🚀 What It Does (60-Second Summary)

1. **User enters** company name or website
2. **App searches** company info using Serper.dev
3. **Crawls** official website for detailed information
4. **Analyzes** with AI (Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, Llama, or custom)
5. **Identifies** 3-5 competitors automatically
6. **Detects** pain points and opportunities
7. **Generates** professional PDF report
8. **Optionally sends** to Discord channel
9. **User downloads** or shares report

**All in ChatGPT-style interface with sidebar settings!**
